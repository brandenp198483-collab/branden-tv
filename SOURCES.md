## Branden TV Goals

- One channel per network whenever possible
- Prefer 1080p
- Prefer 60 FPS
- Prefer Tampa Bay locals
- Keep Philadelphia locals
- Keep Boston locals
- Remove Spanish duplicates
- Remove shopping channels
- Remove religious channels (optional)
- Keep OTA favorites:
  - ABC
  - CBS
  - NBC
  - FOX
